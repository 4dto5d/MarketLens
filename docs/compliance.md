# Compliance Notes

This app is a research, audit, drafting, and CRM tool.

It does not implement:

- robocalling
- prerecorded cold calls
- automatic AI cold calls
- automatic SMS blasting
- CAPTCHA bypass
- private data scraping
- login bypass
- hidden email harvesting
- automatic mass email sending
- fake reviews
- review manipulation
- competitor content copying

Important guardrails:

- Human review is required before outreach.
- Honor opt-outs.
- Use truthful sender information.
- Do not claim a Google Business Profile is unclaimed unless ownership has been manually verified.
- Public data can be incomplete.
- Reports should distinguish observations from conclusions.
- Do not guarantee rankings, revenue, or call volume.
