# Optional intelligence in Guided Opportunity

Intelligence starts Off. Opening a business or saving a worksheet sends no model request. Enable the panel, connect your own ChatGPT account, select an available model and ask a specific question. Review the exact outbound context before choosing **Send once**.

Save current worksheet edits first. Findings are unselected by default, and the saved worksheet is excluded until explicitly included. Selected owner text may be confidential. Original manual AI-observation prompts/responses remain withheld; this feature is reasoning assistance, not automated AI visibility testing.

Focused examines one decision and its decisive unknown. Full Prospect compares the current, narrower, provider and no-sale approaches. Deep Investigation challenges a nominated premise with competing explanations and conditions that would reverse the recommendation. It does not browse, inspect accounts or verify current platform documentation. All modes make one request with no tools or automatic follow-up. Missing source material stays unknown.

Select additional context deliberately: recorded owner goals/capacity, detailed offer reasoning, existing market evidence, self-reported capability, and reviewed service-research methods. All are excluded initially. Structured current owner strategy and application safeguards remain visible as before. Detailed reasoning may contain owner text; inspect the preview. Owner-desired ZIP/city/radius is separate from measured coverage. Research cards describe methods and scope constraints, not business facts, quotes or buying probabilities.

Reasoning effort is selectable for the conservatively supported Sol/Astra models. Account-specific model availability is still required. Unsupported combinations fail before dispatch without substitution. The selected effort is included in the preview, immutable packet and cache identity; it is a request, not proof of effective provider reasoning or a local allowance cap.

Advice now includes a decision brief, deciding sources, decisive unknown and what would change the recommendation. Inspect retained source snapshots beside readable advice. Support checks qualify positive readiness without suitable source support, keep execution blocked by existing Delivery Review requirements and withhold model-generated numeric effort ranges. The original response is retained internally. These checks cannot prove arbitrary prose is true. Explicit operator estimates remain estimates.

History shows current/stale eligibility separately from the original outcome. Rejected advice remains historical and cannot be adopted or returned from cache. New source/policy/card changes and natural market expiry require a new preview. No historical record is rewritten.

Advice distinguishes technical feasibility, operator capability, commercial viability and execution readiness. It does not verify findings, confirm owner instructions, select an offer, quote a price, change qualification or approve contact/delivery. Confirm actual owner facts using Record Contact and existing review routes.

The model cites short labels such as `F1`, `worksheet`, or `delivery_context`. The immutable request snapshot retains the exact finding revision, source hash and application context behind each label. Labels are valid only for that request; the full context hash must still match. Unknown or misspelled labels are rejected, never guessed or repaired automatically. Historical advice from an older interpretation version remains visible but requires a new preview before reuse.

Review individual questions or next actions before appending them to a new worksheet revision. Prior human text is retained. The addition is labeled AI-assisted operator planning. If evidence or the worksheet changed, create a new preview. Noted/rejected reviews and source snapshots remain in retained history. No generated advice enters proposals or outreach automatically.

The local request limit starts at 10 per day and is configurable in the panel. It is shared by this installation's prospect databases and persists across restarts. It is not a provider token/cost cap or a reading of subscription allowance. Failed/ambiguous dispatches consume a local reservation; cancellations may already have used remote resources. Completed exact-context cache hits send no new request. Reuse requires current sources and a maximum age of 24 hours.

Review actual returned usage in retained history and ChatGPT Settings → Usage. No subscription percentages or savings are inferred. A disconnected, unavailable or quota-limited model leaves normal MarketLens workflows available. There is no automatic API-key billing fallback.

Credentials use Windows-protected local storage outside the repository and OneDrive. Evidence snapshots may contain the private material you selected, so protect ordinary database backups. Sign out explicitly to revoke the renewable session; if remote revocation is unconfirmed, disconnect the app in ChatGPT Settings too.

This increment requires independent review. Actual model quality and live account availability must be established by captured tests; deterministic transport fixtures do not establish reasoning quality or commercial outcomes.
