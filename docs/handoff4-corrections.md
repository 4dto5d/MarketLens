The correction pass preserves canonical Offer IDs and the frozen search-evidence contract. It closes provider-work overlap, ZIP coordinate compatibility, payable transitions, known delivery blockers and service-specific territory completeness.

## Offline coordinate compatibility

The user approved a versioned independent offline reference. `assets/postal_geo_census2020_v1.zip` preserves official 2020 Census ZCTA and state cartographic polygons, provenance and source hashes. Runtime validation checks the pinned resource and the candidate coordinate against both relevant polygons before ZIP-centered discovery. Unsupported ZIPs, missing/corrupt reference, incompatible coordinates, holes and ambiguous boundary points fail closed.

This is generalized Census ZCTA compatibility, not a USPS ZIP boundary, ZIP membership, a current ZIP-wide rank or a replacement acquisition center. The original provider center remains the candidate. Radius acquisition remains a circle/window that may cross postal areas. Reference vintage and limitations remain in stored acquisition provenance.

## Scope-specific prerequisite decisions

The manual delivery review records six decisions: ownership authority, change access, provider dependencies, preservation/recovery, acceptance checks and measurement/handoff. Each is `unknown`, `blocked`, `satisfied` or `not_applicable`. Satisfied and not-applicable decisions require scope-specific notes. Facts alone never imply prerequisite satisfaction. Human fact review and owner willingness to buy are separate from technical feasibility.

Known blockers and unknown prerequisite decisions withhold payment readiness. A narrow text edit need not invent DNS migration or provider dependencies; explicitly justified not-applicable decisions remain possible. These decisions are human assertions, not independent proof that a person actually has access.

Old version-1 delivery assertions remain immutable and readable. Missing decisions project as unknown; a new reviewed assertion appends/supersedes instead of rewriting history. No SQL schema change or destructive migration is required. Offer semantic registry version 4 invalidates earlier commercial snapshots for fresh review; canonical IDs stay unchanged.

## Service-specific territory completeness

Coverage rows and selected evidence are scoped to existing service and query intent. Unrelated water-heater or informational observations cannot fill an emergency-plumbing coverage gap. A supported narrower ZIP intervention remains eligible while other desired ZIPs remain explicitly unobserved.
